Online Road Network & Traffic Planner

A road network planning and traffic congestion prediction tool for Nairobi, Kenya — built as a class project.

It loads real road networks from OpenStreetMap, computes routes with Dijkstra and A*, predicts traffic congestion by hour and day, and lets you save and compare different scenarios. Everything runs in the browser.

Sign in About this project

What it does

  • Fetch a live road network for any area by drawing a box on the map
  • Compute the shortest or fastest route with Dijkstra or A*
  • Predict traffic congestion by hour and day of week
  • Get automatic bottleneck and single-point-of-failure insights
  • Animate a simple vehicle simulation on the loaded network
  • Edit roads and save/compare named scenarios

How to use it

  1. Pick a preset area (e.g. CBD) or draw a custom box, then click Fetch
  2. Click two points on the map to set an origin and destination, then Compute
  3. Pick an hour and day, then click Predict to see congestion by segment
  4. Save the current network as a scenario to reload or compare later
